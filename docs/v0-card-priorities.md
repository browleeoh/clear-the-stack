# V0 Hobbit Card Priorities

**Status:** Provisionally approved for Milestone 1 planning  
**Scope:** The first 25 HOB cards proposed for curated beginner guidance and scenarios

This shortlist follows the priority order in the product specification:

1. Cards physically owned or regularly played by the group
2. Cards featuring Hobbit set mechanics
3. Cards with official release-note rulings
4. Cards combining multiple relevant rules concepts
5. Cards likely to generate real beginner questions

Ownership is currently known only for Thorin Oakenshield. The remaining order is evidence-based and provisional. Decklists, card photos, observed play, and real house-game questions may move owned or frequently played cards ahead of the current ranking.

## Mechanic and concept coverage

| Required area | Primary cards | Intended coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storied | Thorin, Bifur, Balin, Kíli, Dáin | Tokens, overlapping characteristics, earning and retaining the designation, timing, and story-dependent effects |
| Enduring story | Thorin, Bifur, Balin, Kíli, Dáin | Earning, retention, loss of the source, and dependent static or triggered effects |
| Recruit | Celebrate, Bard King, Bard the Bowman, Bard's Company, Queen, Mountain-king's Return | Uninterrupted resolution, land versus nonland discard, draw and token modifications, second-card triggers, and multiplayer triggers |
| Hone counters | Dwalin, Sting | Rules-derived bonus, multiple counters, attachment, ability loss, moving Equipment, and targets |
| Landfall | Silvan Reveler, Beorn's Hospitality, Down in the Valley, Dancing from Dark to Dawn | Lands entering by play or effect, trigger timing and order, targets, tokens, Sagas, and persistent type changes |
| Ferocious | Nasty Little Rabbit, The Chief Warg | A condition checked on trigger and resolution contrasted with an attack trigger that is expected to check only when triggered |
| Amass Goblins | Bolg, Azog, Bard King, Goblin Plate Mail, Rhovanion Rampager, Tidings of War, Great Ugly-Looking Goblin | Creating or choosing an Army, multiple Armies, variable X, token modification, 0-toughness concerns, Equipment, and graveyard casting |
| Permanent, token, artifact, legendary | Thorin, Bard King, Celebrate, Dwalin, Sting, Goblin Plate Mail | Qualification, overlapping characteristics, token permanence, attachments, and zone changes |
| Saga and counters | Down in the Valley, Mountain-king's Return, Dwalin, Sting, Nasty Little Rabbit | Lore, hone, and +1/+1 counters; chapter triggers; Saga sacrifice |
| Triggered, static, activated abilities | Bifur, Celebrate, Bolg, Thorin, Bard King, Beorn's Hospitality, Kíli | Identifying each ability type and its timing behavior |
| Target, stack, priority, resolution | Bifur, Celebrate, Sting, Bolg, Azog, Nasty Little Rabbit | Choosing targets, illegal targets, ordering triggers, response windows, and partial or uninterrupted resolution |
| State-based actions | Thorin, Bard King; Mirkwood Meditator as an alternate | Storied timing around an immediately departing permanent, 0-toughness Army tokens, and damage/toughness changes |
| Zones | Celebrate, Silvan Reveler, Tidings of War, Great Ugly-Looking Goblin, Mountain-king's Return | Battlefield, stack, graveyard, exile, and Adventure casting |
| “This way” and “and/or” | Recruit cards, Azog, Tidings; all Storied cards | Causal wording and counting separate qualifying permanents rather than characteristics |

Recruit cases where drawing or discarding is modified or impossible will still need substantial mechanic-level coverage. Hone counters have only two direct cards, so their mechanic page must carry several boundary cases. State-based actions also remain concentrated in a few advanced examples.

## Proposed authoring order for the first 10

1. Thorin Oakenshield — audit and expand the existing curated slice
2. Dwalin, Weaponmaster — establish the hone-counter rules model
3. Sting, Bilbo's Sword — apply that model to targets and attachment
4. Nasty Little Rabbit — establish the Ferocious timing pattern
5. Silvan Reveler — establish Landfall and zone-change behavior
6. Azog, Moria's Ruin — establish baseline Amass and “up to one”
7. Bolg of the North — extend Amass into reflexive triggers and excess damage
8. Celebrate the Mountain-king — establish Recruit, trigger ordering, and zone changes
9. Bard, King of Dale — address replacement effects after Recruit and Amass foundations exist
10. Bifur, Melodic Rider — address the densest Storied and additional-trigger interaction

## Verification requirement

The rules questions, edge cases, and expected interactions in this document are **authoring hypotheses**, not published or verified rulings. During authorship, every outcome must be checked source by source against current Oracle text, the current Comprehensive Rules, official card rulings, the official Hobbit Release Notes and Update Bulletin, and the official mechanics material as applicable. Community discussion may identify useful questions but cannot be the sole authority for an outcome. No hypothesis in this planning document should be marked verified or shipped as player-facing guidance without completing the editorial and verification workflow in the product specification.
